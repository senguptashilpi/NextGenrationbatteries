Technical expertise & characterisation

Hands on the whole workflow.

Synthesis, inert-atmosphere cell assembly, electrochemical testing and structural analysis — plus the instruments and software that tie the data together.

§ 01 — Technical capabilities

Four blocks of bench skill.

Materials Synthesis & Processing

B—01
  • Mechanochemical processing & ball milling
  • Hydrothermal, solvothermal and sol-gel processing
  • Liquid exfoliation, electrospinning and freeze-drying
  • Polymer and ionic-liquid electrolyte formulation

Battery & Device Assembly

B—02
  • Glovebox operation & air-sensitive materials handling (JACOMEX, MBraun) and dry-room facilities
  • Electrode slurry formulation, coating, drying and calendering; gold sputtering
  • Coin-cell assembly & testing (lithium-ion and sodium-ion)
  • Flexible interdigitated electrochemical device fabrication

Electrochemical Characterisation

B—03
  • Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS)
  • Cyclic voltammetry (CV) & galvanostatic charge–discharge (GCD)
  • Rate capability & temperature-dependent ionic conductivity analysis
  • Data modelling and equivalent-circuit fitting

Structural, Surface & Thermal Analysis

B—04
  • X-ray diffraction (XRD) phase analysis
  • X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS)
  • SEM/TEM high-resolution imaging
  • Raman and infrared (FT-IR) spectroscopy
  • BET surface area & porosity measurement
  • Thermal analysis (DSC and TGA)
